# Workgram — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Workgram ("the app") is an unofficial, open-source messaging client that connects to Telegram using the Telegram API. Workgram is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or developed by Telegram.

## No Workgram servers
Workgram has no backend of its own. All messages, contacts, and account data are transmitted to and stored by Telegram and are governed by Telegram's Privacy Policy (https://telegram.org/privacy). We do not operate servers that receive your messages or personal data.

## What we collect
Workgram does not collect, sell, or share personal data. We use no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

## Data stored on your device only
Work features — chat tags and private notes — are stored locally on your device and never leave it. They are not uploaded anywhere.

## Permissions
The app requests access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, and location only to provide messaging features, and only with your consent. This data is used solely for those features and is handled by Telegram as part of the messaging service.

## Account deletion
You can delete your Telegram account at any time from within the app (Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete Account), which removes your data from Telegram's servers.

## Age
Workgram is intended for users aged 17 and older.
